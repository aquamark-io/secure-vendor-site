Program Requirements

  1. 1Be registered with your Secretary of State.
  2. 2Have a company website.
  3. 3Show MFA is enabled for company email.
  4. 4Show MFA is enabled for systems that store sensitive documents or data.
  5. 5Require screens to lock when unattended (setting, written policy, or company instruction).
  6. 6Show offboarding checklist to demonstrate a documented process for promptly and consistently removing access when staff leave or change roles.
  7. 7
    Document and data protection (choose one)
    A
    Documents and data are view-only. Non-management staff can view them but can’t download or export them.
    B
    Documents and data can be downloaded or exported. Non-management staff can download or export them if you use one of the acceptable safeguards.
