Program Requirements

Clear standards. Flexible safeguards. Independent verification. No specific software product or vendor is required.

To qualify

  1. 01Registered business in good standing
  2. 02Company website
  3. 03Service provider in alternative finance
Important

Sensitive information should not be easily extracted from company-controlled systems by non-management personnel without appropriate safeguards.

This applies to both documents and structured data, including applications, bank statements, merchant records, lead information, financial data, and other sensitive business information.

What we verify

Where sensitive information is accessed, processed, and stored, and what prevents non-management personnel from removing unprotected copies from company systems. Demonstrate at least one effective safeguard appropriate to the information and systems being reviewed.

Restricted access

Sensitive documents and data are accessible within company systems, but non-management personnel cannot download, export, copy, or otherwise extract the information.

Examples: view-only access, disabled exports, restricted permissions, and systems that prevent users from extracting underlying data.

Browser and device controls

If downloading or exporting is permitted, company-managed browser and device restrictions prevent unprotected information from leaving the organization’s controlled environment. Controls must address the actual methods of extraction, including file downloads, clipboard transfers, and data exports where applicable.

Examples: managed browsers, endpoint restrictions, disabled USB transfers, controlled downloads, and data loss prevention (DLP) policies.

Document watermarking

Sensitive documents are automatically watermarked before non-management personnel can access or download them. Watermarks should identify the organization or recipient.

Watermarking alone does not satisfy the requirement for structured data that can be exported separately. For example, a CRM that watermarks bank statements but allows employees to export unprotected merchant records or lead lists needs additional safeguards for those exports.

Alternative safeguards

Other methods may be submitted for review. We evaluate whether they accomplish the same objective: preventing or materially restricting the unauthorized extraction of sensitive information.

Examples: monitored environments, virtual desktops, controlled data access, automated handling workflows, or combinations of technical and administrative safeguards. Monitoring or written policies alone do not automatically qualify.

Important clarifications

  • Vendors do not need to implement every safeguard.
  • Different systems or information types may require different safeguards.
  • Downloading and exporting are permitted when appropriate protections are in place.
  • Watermarking is an accepted document safeguard, not a universal solution for structured data.
  • Owners and management are not subject to the same non-management access restrictions under this standard.
  • No specific software product or vendor is required.
  • Alternative approaches can be considered when they meet the underlying objective.

The standard focuses on non-management access, but management exceptions are not an exemption from the organization’s broader responsibility to protect sensitive information.

Verification call

Every applicant participates in a live verification call. The initial call takes approximately 60 minutes, depending on the complexity of your operations.

During the call, we review

  • Where sensitive broker, funder, and merchant information is accessed, processed, and stored
  • Which employees and contractors have access to that information
  • Whether non-management personnel can download, export, copy, or otherwise extract sensitive documents or data
  • Which technical safeguards prevent or restrict unauthorized extraction
  • How those safeguards are implemented and verified

Be prepared to demonstrate your controls through screen sharing, system settings, access permissions, sample workflows, or other appropriate evidence. Descriptions of company policies alone are not sufficient.

Addressing gaps

If a gap is identified, we’ll explain the concern and give you an opportunity to address it. You decide which safeguard best fits your operations, including an alternative approach that meets the same objective. Once the changes are in place, an additional verification call can be scheduled.

More than one call may be needed depending on the complexity of your environment. Certification is granted only after the applicable requirements have been verified.

Certification scope

Certification is issued at the company level, with a clearly defined scope of reviewed operations. For vendors with multiple products, services, or business lines, the assessment identifies which systems or activities were included.
