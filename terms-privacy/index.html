Program Terms & Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

1. Program Overview

Secure Broker™, Secure Funder™, and Secure Vendor™ are independent certification programs operated by Duncan Consulting LLC ("Program Operator") for organizations participating in, or providing services to, the alternative commercial finance industry.

The programs recognize organizations that demonstrate safeguards intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, sharing, or misuse of sensitive business information.

Certification is voluntary, evidence-based, and subject to independent review. Equivalent safeguards may be accepted when they satisfy the intent of a requirement and can be reasonably verified.

No particular technology, vendor, or affiliated service is required for certification. Payment does not influence certification decisions or guarantee approval.

These terms apply to all three programs. "Participant" refers to an organization that enrolls in any of them.

2. Membership Fees & Annual Commitment

The applicable annual membership fees are:

Secure Broker™

  • Fewer than 10 salespeople: $499 annually
  • 10 or more salespeople: $1,499 annually

Secure Funder™

  • All participating funders: $2,499 annually

Secure Vendor™

  • All participating vendors: $2,499 annually

Monthly installments may be offered. Monthly payments are installments toward a noncancelable annual commitment, not a month-to-month membership.

No payment is required to begin enrollment. Applicable fees must be paid, or an approved payment arrangement established, before final certification review.

Secure Vendor™ members who enroll in 2026 are charter members. Their annual term begins January 1, 2027, and they are not charged before that date. For all other members, the annual term begins on their enrollment date.

Participants authorize recurring charges according to their selected payment arrangement. Unless otherwise required by law or agreed in writing, the full annual fee remains payable even if a participant withdraws, stops participating, or fails to qualify for certification.

3. Automatic Renewal & Cancellation

Membership automatically renews for successive annual terms unless canceled before the renewal date.

Before renewal, the Program Operator will email participants at least 30 days in advance, requesting notification of any material changes to their systems, processes, or safeguards since their previous review.

  • No response: Membership renews automatically. Silence does not constitute confirmation that safeguards remain unchanged.
  • Changes reported: The Program Operator may request supporting information or conduct a limited reassessment. Renewal does not guarantee continued certification if applicable requirements are no longer met.
  • Cancellation requested: Membership will not renew. Certification expires at the end of the current term unless previously suspended or revoked.

Participants may cancel future renewal at any time before the renewal date through the billing portal or by emailing info@aquamark.io.

Cancellation does not eliminate outstanding obligations under an existing annual commitment. Material pricing changes will be disclosed before renewal.

4. Certification & Verification

Participants must satisfy the applicable program requirements and successfully complete verification before certification is granted.

For Secure Broker™ and Secure Funder™, verification is conducted through the member portal. Participants provide responses, screenshots, documents, and other evidence demonstrating applicable safeguards.

For Secure Vendor™, verification is conducted through a live screen-sharing call that is not recorded. Participants demonstrate applicable safeguards during the call and may be asked for follow-up evidence.

The Program Operator may request clarification, additional documentation, or other reasonable evidence when necessary.

Certification reflects the safeguards demonstrated during the review period and does not establish ongoing or continuous compliance.

5. Enrollment Timeline

Participants may enroll before all required safeguards are implemented.

Verification must be completed within 90 days of enrollment unless an extension is approved.

Failure to complete verification within the applicable period may result in removal of pending directory listings or closure of the application.

Extensions may be granted at the Program Operator's discretion. Extensions do not automatically change membership fees or payment obligations.

6. Participant Responsibilities

Participants agree to:

  1. Provide truthful, accurate, and complete information.
  2. Submit only information they are authorized to disclose.
  3. Demonstrate applicable safeguards in good faith.
  4. Notify the Program Operator of material changes affecting certification.
  5. Correct material inaccuracies discovered in previously submitted information.
  6. Cooperate reasonably with verification and reassessment requests.
  7. Maintain the safeguards required for continued certification.

Participants remain responsible for their employees, business practices, systems, information security, and regulatory obligations.

False statements, falsified evidence, or intentional misrepresentations may result in denial or revocation of certification without refund.

7. Certification Decisions, Suspension & Revocation

The Program Operator retains authority to approve, deny, suspend, revoke, or decline renewal of certification based on applicable requirements and available evidence.

Certification may be reassessed when credible information raises concerns about continued compliance, material inaccuracies, misuse of certification marks, or the protection of sensitive information.

Pending litigation or allegations do not automatically establish misconduct. The Program Operator may consider the nature, credibility, and relevance of such matters when determining whether further review or temporary suspension is warranted.

When reasonably appropriate, participants will be given an opportunity to provide clarification or corrective action before a final adverse decision.

The Program Operator may take immediate temporary action when reasonably necessary to prevent misleading representations or protect program integrity.

Certification decisions will be made in good faith and according to program standards. Nothing in these terms requires the Program Operator to grant or maintain certification when applicable requirements have not been demonstrated.

8. No Continuous Monitoring

Certification reflects a limited assessment based on evidence made available during the review period.

The Program Operator does not undertake continuous monitoring of participants or assume responsibility for supervising their business operations, employees, or information-security practices.

The Program Operator is not obligated to independently investigate every allegation or identify subsequent misconduct, security incidents, or changes in safeguards.

Additional reviews may be conducted at the Program Operator's discretion or as required by an expressly agreed program procedure.

9. Public Directory & Certification Marks

By enrolling, participants authorize publication of their organization's name, logo, participation status, certification status, and program designations (such as charter member) in program directories and related communications.

The Program Operator may announce enrollment, certification, suspension, expiration, or revocation using accurate and appropriately qualified language.

Certified participants receive a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to display approved certification marks while their certification remains active.

Participants may not alter certification marks, imply regulatory endorsement, or misrepresent the scope or status of certification.

Upon suspension, expiration, or revocation, participants must promptly discontinue representations of active certification and remove or update affected badges and references.

The Program Operator retains ownership of its names, logos, certification marks, and related intellectual property.

10. Confidentiality

Non-public information obtained during verification will be treated as confidential and used only for verification, program administration, quality control, certification decisions, and related legitimate operations.

Access is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers with a legitimate need to process the information, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is publicly available without breach, independently obtained lawfully, or required to be disclosed by applicable law or legal process.

The Program Operator will not publicly disclose internal policies, screenshots, proprietary documentation, or detailed assessment findings without authorization, except as legally required.

11. Evidence Storage, AI Processing & Deletion

Verification materials are stored using Supabase infrastructure, with evidence access restricted to authorized program administrators through the Program Operator's application controls. Secure Vendor™ verification calls are not recorded; only reviewer notes and any materials the participant chooses to provide are retained.

Anthropic's commercial API is used to assist with summarizing and analyzing submitted evidence. Materials processed this way are not used to train AI models. AI-assisted outputs support the review process but do not independently determine certification outcomes. Final decisions are made by authorized human reviewers.

Participants should redact unnecessary personal, financial, and proprietary information before submission.

Original verification evidence is generally retained unless deletion is requested or the Program Operator determines it is no longer necessary.

Participants may request deletion of original verification materials at any time. Subject to legal obligations, security needs, pending disputes, and legitimate recordkeeping requirements, approved requests will result in deletion of original materials from active program storage.

The Program Operator may retain written assessment summaries, certification decisions, administrative records, and other information reasonably necessary to document the review, administer membership, or protect legal rights.

Deletion of original evidence does not ordinarily invalidate an existing certification, provided sufficient assessment records remain. Requests affecting an active review may limit the ability to complete or maintain verification.

Information processed by third-party providers is subject to their applicable contractual data-protection and retention arrangements.

The Program Operator maintains reasonable security measures but cannot guarantee that electronic systems are entirely free from security risks.

12. Refund Policy

Program fees support administration, verification, member services, directory maintenance, and related operations. They do not purchase a guaranteed certification outcome.

Verification begins when the Program Operator undertakes substantive review of submitted evidence.

Once substantive verification begins, membership fees are nonrefundable, including when certification is denied, revoked, or the participant fails to complete the process, except as required by law.

Refund requests made before substantive review begins may be considered based on services already provided and applicable circumstances.

Cancellation of automatic renewal prevents future annual commitments but does not cancel outstanding installments for the current membership term.

The Program Operator may issue discretionary refunds or credits without establishing an obligation to provide them in other circumstances.

13. Nature & Limits of Certification

Certification represents a limited determination that an organization demonstrated alignment with applicable program requirements at the time of review.

Certification is not:

  • A regulatory examination or legal opinion
  • A comprehensive cybersecurity audit
  • A guarantee of legal or regulatory compliance
  • An endorsement of an organization's financial condition or overall business practices
  • A guarantee against data theft, unauthorized sharing, fraud, or misconduct
  • A guarantee of future performance or continued compliance

Participants remain solely responsible for their operations and personnel.

Certification does not replace independent due diligence. Third parties should not rely on certification as a guarantee of security, compliance, or business conduct.

Certification does not create a contractual, fiduciary, supervisory, or advisory relationship between the Program Operator and third parties.

Privacy Policy

19. Information We Collect

Duncan Consulting LLC collects information reasonably necessary to administer Secure Broker™, Secure Funder™, and Secure Vendor™, including:

  • Organization and business contact information
  • Account and authentication information
  • Verification responses, documents, screenshots, and supporting evidence
  • Assessment summaries and certification records
  • Billing and membership information
  • Communications and support requests
  • Technical information needed for portal functionality and security

Payment information may be processed by authorized payment providers rather than stored directly by Duncan Consulting LLC.

20. How We Collect & Use Information

Information is collected through program websites, member portals, enrollment forms, verification calls, billing systems, and direct communications.

Information is used for:

  • Membership administration and billing
  • Evidence review and certification decisions
  • Program communications and support
  • Directory maintenance and authorized announcements
  • Security, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution
  • Compliance with applicable legal obligations

Personal information is not sold. Verification materials are not used for unrelated advertising.

21. Service Providers & AI Processing

Duncan Consulting LLC uses authorized service providers to support program operations.

Verification materials are stored through Supabase. Authorized program administrators access submitted evidence for review and administration.

Anthropic's commercial API processes relevant evidence to assist with summarization and analysis. Human reviewers make final certification decisions.

Additional service providers may support website hosting, communications, billing, and security.

Information is shared with these providers only as reasonably necessary for their services, subject to applicable contractual and legal protections.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect legal rights, or in connection with a lawful organizational transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

22. Evidence Retention & Deletion

Approved requests result in deletion from active program storage, subject to applicable legal requirements, legitimate recordkeeping needs, and technical limitations.

Assessment summaries, certification decisions, billing records, and other necessary administrative information may be retained for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Information will not be retained longer than reasonably necessary for its disclosed purposes, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Duncan Consulting LLC uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect submitted information.
