    Watermarking. Documents are automatically watermarked with your company name or logo before non-management staff can open or download them. Aquamark or an equivalent service is acceptable. Why watermarking counts: Visible watermarks are reasonable deterrents. By marking every page with your company’s name, they discourage unauthorized sharing or misuse because the documents can be traced back to their source.
    B
    Browser and device controls. Block personal email and cloud storage websites, and block USB transfers.

    Data exports (choose one)
    A
    Browser and device controls. Same as 1B. Watermarking doesn’t cover exported data.
    B
    Restricted exports or other controls. Exports are disabled or limited, or work happens in a controlled environment such as a virtual desktop. We review these on the verification call.
