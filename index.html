Our Mission

Secure Vendor™ recognizes technology and service providers that take steps to protect sensitive broker, funder, and merchant information, giving the businesses they serve a clearer signal about which vendors prioritize data protection.

Who can participate

Any technology or service provider that accesses, collects, processes, stores, or transmits sensitive information in alternative finance.

  • CRMs and loan origination systems
  • Broker and ISO portals
  • Marketplaces and lead providers
  • Bank statement and underwriting analytics
  • Document collection and e-signature
  • Data, verification, and AI providers
  • Payment and servicing platforms
  • Outsourced processing and support services
Different by design

No checklist. A review built around your business.

Every vendor operates differently, so there’s no fixed questionnaire. We learn how your company handles sensitive information, then agree with you on what to review.

FAQ

Common questions about Secure Vendor.

What is Secure Vendor?

An independent certification that recognizes technology and service providers taking steps to protect sensitive broker, funder, and merchant information in alternative finance. We don’t sell software or services, and Secure Broker and Secure Funder members aren’t required to use certified vendors.

What types of vendors can participate?

Any technology or service provider that accesses, collects, processes, stores, or transmits sensitive information in alternative finance. That includes:

  • CRMs, loan origination systems, and broker or ISO portals
  • Marketplaces and lead providers
  • Bank statement and underwriting analytics
  • Document collection and e-signature
  • Data, verification, and AI providers
  • Payment and servicing platforms
  • Outsourced processing and support services

Not sure if you fit? Ask us.

Does certification guarantee that a vendor is secure?

No. Certification recognizes that a vendor has taken steps to protect sensitive information and that those safeguards were independently reviewed. It isn’t a guarantee of security, and it doesn’t replace your own due diligence when choosing a vendor.

Why aren’t there set requirements?

Because every vendor operates differently. A CRM, a lead provider, and an outsourced processing team handle information in very different ways, and a single questionnaire wouldn’t fit any of them well.

The expectations stay the same for every vendor: protect the sensitive information you’re trusted with. What varies is the safeguards you use and the evidence that shows them. We scope each review to your business and agree on it with you up front.

What do you look at?

It depends on your business. Common starting points include:

  • How people sign in and how access is granted and removed
  • Roles, permissions, and download controls
  • Activity and audit logging
  • How information is separated, retained, and deleted
  • Whether information is shared, resold, or reused
  • Third parties that touch sensitive information

Not every area applies, and we may add others that fit your business. See how it works.

Do you accept SOC 2 or ISO 27001 reports?

Yes, as supporting evidence. They aren’t required to participate, and they don’t determine certification on their own. We’ll focus the review on what they don’t cover, like how sensitive information moves through your business in alternative finance.

Do you need access to our code or systems?

No. Most evidence is screenshots, documentation, or a walkthrough of how you work. You can redact personal or proprietary information.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. We’ll set a timeline together on the scoping call, and you can move at your own pace. Support is available throughout.

How does renewal work?

Certification renews annually. If your services or processes have changed in ways that affect sensitive information, you report the changes for review. If nothing has changed, you confirm that, and payment renews automatically.

Are refunds available?

No refunds are available once the review has begun, regardless of the outcome.

Can certification be revoked?

Yes, if the safeguards reviewed are no longer maintained or information provided during the review is found to be inaccurate. If certification is revoked or lapses, the badge and any mention of certified status must be removed.

Christina Duncan

Christina Duncan has worked in alternative business finance since 2009, including serving as COO of two working capital brokerages, one with an in-house funding division and more than 100 employees.

In 2025, she founded Aquamark to address data and deal theft in the industry. That work led to Secure Funder™, Secure Broker™, and Secure Vendor™, independent certification programs focused on practical standards for protecting merchant information.

Today, Christina’s work focuses on advancing transparency and accountability across the industry, advocating for measures such as broker registration, and helping create an environment where small business owners can seek alternative capital with greater confidence and better outcomes.
