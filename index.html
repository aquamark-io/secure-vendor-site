FAQ

Common questions about Secure Vendor.

What is Secure Vendor?

An independent certification that recognizes technology and service providers taking steps to protect sensitive broker, funder, and merchant information in alternative finance. We don’t sell software or services, and Secure Broker and Secure Funder members aren’t required to use certified vendors.

Why get certified?
  • Earn trust: show partners and customers that you’ve taken steps to protect the information they share with you.
  • Stand apart: demonstrate your commitment through independent verification.
  • Gain recognition: receive a digital badge, public directory listing, and announcement recognizing your company’s certification.
What types of vendors can participate?

Any technology or service provider that accesses, collects, processes, stores, or transmits sensitive information in alternative finance. That includes:

  • CRM and loan origination systems
  • Document processing and services
  • Lead and data providers
  • Payment and servicing platforms
  • Outsourced support services
  • Marketplaces
  • Fraud detection and verification
  • Underwriting and analytics

Not sure if you fit? Ask us.

How does the verification process work?

Every applicant participates in a live verification call, typically lasting about an hour. We’ll identify where your company stores sensitive documents and data, particularly where non-management personnel have access, and ask you to demonstrate the safeguards in place to prevent unprotected information from leaving company systems. Additional calls can be scheduled if needed.

What should we prepare for the verification call?

Be prepared to walk us through the systems where sensitive broker, funder, or merchant information is handled and demonstrate the safeguards in place. This may include screen sharing, access permissions, system configurations, or sample workflows.

What safeguards are accepted?

Accepted safeguards include restricted access, managed browser and device controls, document watermarking, and alternative protections that meet the same data protection objective. Different safeguards may be appropriate for different systems and information types. See the requirements.

Can employees still download documents or export data?

Yes. Downloading and exporting are permitted when appropriate safeguards are in place. Documents may be protected through watermarking or technical restrictions. Structured data exports require controls appropriate to the information being extracted.

Does watermarking satisfy the requirements?

Watermarking is an accepted safeguard for documents when implemented before non-management personnel can access or download unprotected copies. It does not automatically protect underlying data that can be exported separately.

Are owners and management subject to the same restrictions?

No. The specific extraction safeguards focus on non-management personnel. Owners and management may retain broader access, although companies remain responsible for protecting sensitive information throughout their operations.

What if our company uses a different approach?

We recognize that vendors operate differently. If your company uses safeguards not listed in our guidelines, contact us. We can review alternative approaches that accomplish the same data protection objective.

What happens if we don’t meet a requirement?

We’ll explain the gap and give your team an opportunity to address it. You can choose the safeguard that works best for your operations, including an alternative approach that meets the same objective. Once implemented, we can schedule another call to verify the changes.

Can we complete certification in one call?

Yes. If all applicable safeguards can be verified during the initial call, no additional verification call may be necessary. More complex environments or outstanding items may require follow-up.

Do we have to use a specific technology or solution?

No. Secure Vendor is technology-neutral. We evaluate whether your safeguards meet the certification objectives, not which products or providers you use.

Do we need SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification?

No. Existing security certifications may provide useful supporting evidence, but they are not required. Secure Vendor independently evaluates the safeguards relevant to its certification standards.

Is certification guaranteed once we join?

No. Certification is awarded only after the applicable safeguards have been independently verified. Applicants are given an opportunity to address identified gaps, but membership does not guarantee approval.

Does certification guarantee our company is secure?

No. Certification recognizes verified safeguards within the reviewed scope. It does not guarantee that a company is immune to data theft, unauthorized access, or security incidents.

How does renewal work?

Certification renews annually. If your services or processes have changed in ways that affect sensitive information, you report the changes for review. If nothing has changed, you confirm that, and payment renews automatically.

When is payment due?

When you schedule your verification call.

Are refunds available?

No refunds are available once the review has begun, regardless of the outcome.

Can certification be revoked?

Yes, if the safeguards reviewed are no longer maintained or information provided during the review is found to be inaccurate. If certification is revoked or lapses, the badge and any mention of certified status must be removed.
