Our Mission

Secure Vendor™ recognizes technology and other service providers taking steps to prevent deal theft, also known as backdooring. That recognition makes it harder for bad actors to blend in and strengthens trust and accountability across alternative commercial finance.

  • Lead and data providers

FAQ

What is the value of certification?

Show customers and partners that your data protection safeguards have been independently verified. Certified companies receive a digital badge, a public directory listing, and a certification announcement on the program’s social media channels and in its newsletter.

How does certification help prevent backdooring?

We verify that companies have safeguards in place that make it harder for staff to take sensitive documents and data out of company systems and send them to themselves for resale.

What are the requirements?

Registered with your Secretary of State, have a website, and meet all of the program’s technical requirements. See the technical requirements here.

How does verification work?

We’ll meet with you on a live screen-sharing call that typically lasts 30 to 90 minutes. The call isn’t recorded. During the call, you’ll show us where non-management staff can access sensitive documents or data and what safeguards you use to protect that information. You can pause screen sharing between questions to keep sensitive or proprietary information private.

Can we join before we meet the requirements?

Yes, we encourage it. You have 90 days to meet the requirements. You can ask for more time or book a free call for help. We’ll certify your company once we confirm you meet all of the requirements.

How much does it cost, and when is payment due?

$2,499 a year. You can pay monthly. Your first payment is due when you schedule your verification call.

Can staff still download documents?

Yes, as long as you have appropriate safeguards in place.

How does renewal work?

Certification renews every year. We’ll email you to ask if your systems or the way you store or protect sensitive data have changed. Tell us what changed so we can review it. If nothing has changed, confirm that and your payment will renew automatically.
