Our Mission

Secure Software recognizes technology companies that protect merchant data across alternative finance, giving brokers, funders, and the small businesses they serve a clear signal about which platforms they can trust.

FAQ

Common questions about Secure Software.

What is Secure Software?

An independent certification that recognizes software companies taking steps to protect merchant data in alternative finance. We don’t sell software, and Secure Broker and Secure Funder don’t require any specific product.

Why aren’t there set requirements?

Because every platform is different. A CRM, an underwriting tool, and a lead marketplace handle data in very different ways, and a single questionnaire wouldn’t fit any of them well. Instead, we scope each review to your product and agree on it with you up front.

What do you look at?

It depends on your product. Common starting points include:

  • How users sign in and how access is granted and removed
  • Roles, permissions, and download controls
  • Activity and audit logging
  • How customer data is separated, retained, and deleted
  • Third-party providers that touch merchant data

Not every area applies, and we may add others that fit your product. See how it works.

Do you accept SOC 2 or ISO 27001 reports?

Yes. Existing reports and certifications can cover a lot of ground, and we’ll focus the review on what they don’t, like how merchant data moves through your product in alternative finance.

Do you need access to our code or systems?

No. Most evidence is screenshots, documentation, or a walkthrough of your product. You can redact personal or proprietary information.

How long does it take?

It depends on scope. We’ll set a timeline together on the scoping call, and you can move at your own pace. Support is available throughout.

How does renewal work?

Certification renews annually. If your product or processes have changed in ways that affect merchant data, you report the changes for review. If nothing has changed, you confirm that, and payment renews automatically.

Are refunds available?

No refunds are available once the review has begun, regardless of the outcome.

Can certification be revoked?

Yes, if the safeguards reviewed are no longer maintained or information provided during the review is found to be inaccurate. If certification is revoked or lapses, the badge and any mention of certified status must be removed.

Christina Duncan

Christina Duncan has worked in alternative business finance since 2009, including serving as COO of two working capital brokerages, one with an in-house funding division and more than 100 employees.

In 2025, she founded Aquamark to address data and deal theft in the industry. That work led to Secure Funder™, Secure Broker™, and Secure Software™, independent certification programs focused on practical standards for protecting merchant information.

Today, Christina’s work focuses on advancing transparency and accountability across the industry, advocating for measures such as broker registration, and helping create an environment where small business owners can seek alternative capital with greater confidence and better outcomes.
